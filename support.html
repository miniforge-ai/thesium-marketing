Frequently asked questions

Where does Thesium store my portfolio data?

Locally on your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/Thesium/. It's never transmitted off-device. See the privacy policy for details.

What CSV format does the portfolio importer expect?

Thesium reads the standard Fidelity portfolio export format (Accounts → Positions → Download from the Fidelity web portal). Other brokers' exports may work if the column headers happen to match; if your broker isn't recognized, email us a sample (with personal info redacted) and we'll add support.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple. Open System Settings → Apple Account → Media & Purchases → Subscriptions to manage or cancel. Thesium itself doesn't store payment information and can't issue refunds; Apple's Report a Problem page handles refund requests.

Why is my portfolio missing from the Portfolio tab?

The cache lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Thesium/. macOS may relocate Application Support contents during a Time Machine restore or migration; if your portfolio disappears, re-import the CSV from Settings → Portfolio Management → Re-import Portfolio.

What does the "Confidence" column mean on the Risk page?

Each risk family's confidence rating reflects how recently its underlying data was published. Some indicators (FRED weekly series) update once a week; others (daily market series) update every market close. Low confidence means we're working with stale data and the bucket may shift when the next publication arrives.

I found a bug.

Email support@miniforge.ai with steps to reproduce. If it's a crash, the macOS crash report (Console.app → Crash Reports) is especially helpful.

System requirements

  • macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later — Apple Silicon and Intel both supported.
  • About 50 MB of disk space.
  • An internet connection for the daily snapshot refresh (the app works offline against the bundled snapshot).

Privacy & data

Thesium does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. See the privacy policy for the full disclosure.
