Portfolio risk intelligence, on your Mac.

Thesium pulls macro regime indicators, family-level risk signals, and your own portfolio into one local-first dashboard. Your holdings stay on your Mac. The picture refreshes every market day.

Free · macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon & Intel

What it does

Four pieces, working together — in one window, on your machine.

Macro regime, at a glance

A composite regime score plus risk-family buckets — valuation, credit spreads, liquidity, employment, and more — each with a transparent confidence rating.

Drill into any family

Open a family to see the leaf metrics behind its bucket: Shiller CAPE, HY OAS, put/call ratio, SLOS tightening, and the rest — sourced and timestamped.

Your portfolio in context

Import a Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, E*TRADE, Ameriprise, or Empower export to see exposures against the current regime. Your CSV stays on your Mac.

Heimdall signal engine

Watches the snapshot diff and flags regime transitions, family bucket changes, and concentration risk — so the picture doesn't quietly change underneath you.

Thesium Pro

Live data and research lenses.

Pro adds daily snapshot refresh, deeper research lenses across Value, Dividend & Income, Quality, Growth, and Formula families, and the live research catalog. The free tier ships the full macro & portfolio dashboard with weekly snapshots.

Pro is an in-app subscription, billed by Apple. Manage or cancel from System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Privacy

Sandboxed and local-first. Outbound traffic is limited to fetching market snapshots; no portfolio data leaves your Mac. Read the full policy →
