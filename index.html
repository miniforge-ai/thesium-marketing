Portfolio risk intelligence, on your Mac.

Thesium pulls macro regime indicators, family-level risk signals, and your own portfolio into one local-first dashboard. Your holdings stay on your Mac. The picture refreshes every market day.

What it does

  • A live macro snapshot — composite regime score, risk family buckets (valuation, credit spreads, liquidity, employment, more), and a transparent confidence rating.
  • Drill into any risk family to see the leaf metrics driving its bucket: Shiller CAPE, HY OAS, put/call ratio, SLOS tightening, and the rest.
  • Import a Fidelity portfolio CSV to see asset-class exposures and where you sit relative to the current regime.
  • Heimdall — a signal engine that watches the snapshot diff and flags regime transitions, family bucket changes, and concentration risk.

Privacy

Thesium is sandboxed and runs entirely on your Mac. The app makes outbound connections only to fetch market snapshots from our backend; no portfolio data, holdings, or behavior is ever sent off-device. See the full privacy policy for the specifics.
