Thesium Research

An answer is only as good as the record behind it.

Thesium Research runs research tasks that take minutes to hours and hundreds of external observations. Everything the run learns lands in an append-only ledger, so the answer arrives with its claim graph, its citations, its unresolved conflicts, and a record of every action taken to get there.

The shape

Agents propose. The control plane decides.

Research agents hold no ambient authority. They read a deterministic projection of a shared workspace and submit proposed changes. A request that would reach outside — fetch a page, call an API, write a file — states its intent, and the control plane validates the proposal, evaluates policy, reserves budget, grants one narrow capability, runs the effect inside an enclosure, and records the observation as evidence.

The point of that structure is not caution for its own sake. It is what makes the run reproducible: the ledger is the answer's provenance, not a log written beside it.

While it runs

You are not locked out of the loop.

Watch it work

Structured progress, plus the open questions, claims, evidence, and conflicts as they accumulate. Not a spinner.

Approve what matters

Sensitive actions pause for a decision. You approve or deny them individually, and the choice is recorded.

Pause, resume, reopen

A run has a durable identity. It survives a restart, and the result can be reopened later without depending on whatever worker happened to produce it.
