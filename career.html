Thesium Career

Your resume is the weakest evidence you have.

The work that actually shows what you can do is spread across project documents, code, architecture decisions, writing, and reviews. Thesium Career reads those, builds an evidence portfolio from them, and scores roles and growth decisions against it.

In development · closed beta

Ask about the beta

Why it exists

Job platforms model people badly.

Keyword matching, title similarity, and self-entered profile fields are weak representations of a person. They bury the small set of roles someone is unusually well suited for, and they handle non-linear senior careers worst of all — because the evidence for seniority lives in artifacts those systems never read.

Thesium Career is not a mass-apply tool and not a keyword optimizer. It is a private fit-analysis engine: it identifies the narrow frontier of roles you fit and explains, with citations back to your own work, why.

Surfaces

Three views on one model.

Research

Roles ranked against your evidence portfolio, each with a fit score and the specific evidence items that produced it. Weak or missing evidence is called out rather than smoothed over.

Lenses

Grounded outputs for one specific role: a tailored resume, a narrative, an interview prep pack. Every claim traces back to an evidence item, and you can override what gets selected.

Growth Path

Your own growth rubric, scored against your evidence, with projections shown as ranges rather than single numbers. Projections are labeled as projections.
