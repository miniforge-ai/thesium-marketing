Thesium Risk

Portfolio risk intelligence, on your Mac.

Thesium Risk pulls macro regime indicators, risk-family signals, and your own portfolio into one dashboard. Your holdings stay on your Mac. The picture refreshes every market day.

Free · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon & Intel

Listed on the Mac App Store as Thesium.

What it does

Four pieces, one window.

Macro regime, at a glance

A composite regime score plus risk-family buckets — valuation, credit spreads, liquidity, employment, and more — each carrying a confidence rating you can see.

Drill into any family

Open a family to see the leaf metrics behind its bucket: Shiller CAPE, HY OAS, put/call ratio, SLOS tightening, and the rest — sourced and timestamped.

Your portfolio in context

Import a Fidelity, Schwab, Vanguard, E*TRADE, Ameriprise, or Empower export to see exposures against the current regime. Your CSV stays on your Mac.

Heimdall signal engine

Watches the snapshot diff and flags regime transitions, family bucket changes, and concentration risk — so the picture doesn't quietly change underneath you.

Thesium Risk Pro

Live data and research lenses.

Pro adds daily snapshot refresh, deeper research lenses across Value, Dividend & Income, Quality, Growth, and Formula families, and the live research catalog. The free tier ships the full macro and portfolio dashboard with weekly snapshots.

Pro is an in-app subscription, billed by Apple. Manage or cancel from System Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions.

Thesium Risk is an information tool, not investment advice. Miniforge AI is not a registered investment adviser, broker-dealer, or financial planner, and nothing the app produces is a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Regime scores and risk-family buckets are derived from public data that may be stale, revised, or wrong. You are responsible for your own investment decisions.
