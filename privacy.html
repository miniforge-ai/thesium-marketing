Privacy Policy

Thesium does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The short version: your data stays on your Mac.

What we collect

Nothing. Thesium has no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party data partners.

Portfolio data

If you import a portfolio CSV, it's read by Thesium via macOS's file picker and cached locally at:

~/Library/Application Support/Thesium/

Portfolio data — holdings, account names, ticker symbols, lot sizes, cost basis, gain/loss — is never transmitted off your Mac. It is not sent to our servers, not synced to the cloud, and not shared with any third party. Removing the app removes the data; you can also clear it manually from Settings → Portfolio Management → Delete Portfolio.

Network requests

The app makes outbound network requests for two purposes only:

  1. Market snapshot fetch. Thesium downloads a publicly-available JSON snapshot of macro and risk-family data from thesium-api.christopher-54d.workers.dev (a Cloudflare Worker we operate). These requests are unauthenticated and contain no user identifier.
  2. Subscription validation. If you subscribe to Thesium Pro through the Mac App Store, Apple's StoreKit framework verifies the receipt with Apple's servers. We do not see, store, or process payment information; that's handled entirely by Apple.

No other network traffic originates from the app.

Diagnostics

The app does not collect crash reports or diagnostic logs. macOS's system-wide crash reporter (which you can opt into via System Settings) may forward anonymized crashes to Apple, who may share them with us in aggregate App Analytics; that channel is governed by your Apple privacy settings, not by Thesium.

Children

Thesium is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change and the prior version will remain available in our git history at github.com/miniforge-ai/thesium.
